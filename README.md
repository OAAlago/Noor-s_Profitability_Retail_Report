### Data Analysis and Insights
1. Identify top performing states
    - The average total profit across all states was 1260.80 Rupees. States exceeding this benchmark included Maharashtra, Madha Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh.
2. Determine most profitable category
   - Clothing was the most profitable category indicating stronger financial performance within the category.
3. Analyze sales trends over time
    - Sales performance varied considerably across the period analyzed. This indicates that sales were not consistent throughout the period.
4. Identify most profitable sub-category
   - Printers were the most profitable sub-category showing a wide difference in profitability across products.


### Recommendations
Based on the analysis, we recommend the following actions
- Focus marketing and sales effort on top performing states while developing strategies to improve performance in lower performing regions.
- Prioritize investment in the most profitable product categories and sub-categories.
- Investigate the reasons behind losses in certain categories and implement cost control measures such as negotiating better prices with suppliers, reviewing discount and minimizing logistics cost to improve overall profitability.
- Sales and profit should be monitored continuously, as profit provides a more accurate measure of business performance than sales alone.

- ### Limitations
- The dataset only covers transactions from April 2018 to March 2019. As a result, findings may not reflect long term sales trends.
- The dataset has limited customer information limiting customer behavior analysis.
- The analysis is limited to the state and cities present in the dataset and may not represent the company’s performance in other regions.
- There is no information on whether sales were made through physical stores, online platforms, or other channels.
